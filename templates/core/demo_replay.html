{% elif scenario_key == 'timeout_retry' %}

A transient error permits a scheduled retry, up to three attempts per cycle. Every outgoing attempt needs a daily budget slot; a retained snapshot does not become fresh because a retry was scheduled.

{% elif scenario_key == 'circuit_recovery' %}

Three consecutive transient failures open the provider circuit. After the 15-minute cool-off, one half-open probe may run. Success closes the circuit; another transient failure reopens it.
